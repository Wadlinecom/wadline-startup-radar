Three machine-readable files from Wadline, an independent B2B directory of IT service providers operating since 2015.

rates.json — median hourly rate charged by IT companies, by country, derived from the rates companies publish on their own profiles rather than from a survey. Each country carries the sample size behind it, and countries below the minimum sample are left out rather than reported thin.

salaries.json — IT salary ranges by city and by technology, in US dollars, unadjusted for local prices or tax.

startups/data.json — liveness state for 99 tracked startups, measured from public signals on each startup's own infrastructure: sitemap freshness, changelog and release activity, job postings. The file includes the signal definitions with their weights and freshness windows, so the scoring can be reproduced rather than taken on trust. Current totals: 79 growing, 2 steady, 1 quiet, 13 dormant, 4 unresolved.

Every figure carries the date it was generated. The company catalogue and the client reviews behind these aggregates are not published.

Method: wadline.com/methodology
